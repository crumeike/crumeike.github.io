About Me

I am an AI researcher whose work spans multimodal vision-language models, scientific imaging, computer vision, and the development of intelligent systems that support real-world decision-making. My research focuses on building models that enhance understanding in science, engineering, and community resilience. I work at the intersection of data-driven learning and practical applications, creating methods that are both technically rigorous and meaningful to society.

I have conducted research at Argonne National Laboratory through two appointments in 2024 and 2025, where I contributed to projects involving multimodal models for scientific discovery and biomedical imaging. My work has also addressed post-disaster building damage assessment, urban resilience, sensor-driven flood monitoring, and AI approaches for low-data scientific environments.

Projects and Research Areas

Multimodal Foundation Models for Science

Developed domain-adapted vision-language models for scientific and biomedical imaging. My work includes adapting general-purpose foundation models to low-data scientific domains, designing vision-language systems for ptychography and radiation imaging, applying reinforcement learning to autonomous scientific workflows, and scaling training across HPC and multi-GPU environments.

AI for Post-Disaster Damage Assessment

Created end-to-end computer vision systems for rapid and reliable building damage estimation. This work includes damage detection using models such as YOLO, SSD, and RT-DETR for tornado and windstorm events, integrating imagery with geospatial data, designing multimodal deep learning methods for damage assessment, and developing automated systems that reduce evaluation time from weeks to minutes.

AI for Biomedical Imaging

Advanced vision-language and multimodal models for biomedical image interpretation and low-dose imaging. This includes developing biomedical VLMs, improving low-dose radiation therapy imaging pipelines through contextual feature extraction, and designing AI systems that support clinical and scientific workflows.

Smart Cities and IoT Resilience Systems

Developed intelligent sensing and decision-support tools for urban infrastructure, particularly in the context of flooding and resilience. My work includes IoT flood monitoring networks, edge computing for real-time environmental sensing, city-scale resilience modeling, and integrating standards-based data architectures for monitoring civil infrastructure systems.
